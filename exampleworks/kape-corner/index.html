Our story

A small corner with a big love for coffee

Kape Corner started as a tiny coffee cart with one grinder and a lot of heart. Today we are a neighborhood café where students, freelancers and families slow down, catch up and enjoy a good cup.

We work with Philippine farms, roast in small batches and bake what we serve, because good coffee deserves good company.
