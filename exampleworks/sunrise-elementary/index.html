Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What are the school hours?

Classes run from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday. The office is open from 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

What ages do you accept?

Kindergarten accepts children who turn 5 by the start of the school year. Grades 1 to 6 follow the standard age levels.

Can I visit before enrolling?

Yes. Send us an inquiry and we will schedule a campus tour that fits your day.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Tuition can be paid in full, per semester or monthly. Our office will walk you through the options.
