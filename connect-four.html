Connect Four

Drop a disc. Line up four. Win.

See how it works

Two ways to play

Pick a mode from the menu and the game starts right away.

Player Vs. Player

Two people share one phone and take turns dropping discs.

Player Vs. Bot

No one to play with? Take on the computer by yourself.

How to play

  1. 1

    Choose Player Vs. Player or Player Vs. Bot on the menu.

  2. 2

    Tap a column to drop your disc. It falls to the lowest empty spot.

  3. 3

    Be the first to line up four discs across, up and down, or diagonally.
