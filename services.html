How we work

Each project moves through the same five steps, whichever package you choose.

  1. Brief and planning

    You choose a package and send us your text, images and any brand colors or logo. We confirm the number of pages and the features before work starts.

  2. Design

    We set up the page layouts, color palette and typography, so the structure and look of the site are clear before the code is written.

  3. Development

    We build each page in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with the contact form and the other features included in your package.

  4. Testing

    We check every link, form and page in different browsers and on phone, tablet and desktop widths, and fix whatever we find.

  5. Review and handover

    You review the site and send your changes as one list for each revision round in your package. Once you approve, we hand over the finished site.

Common questions

What is not included in the prices?

All prices are one-time fees in PHP. The domain name and hosting are not included. Contact us and we can go over your options for both.

What counts as a revision round?

A revision round is one list of changes that you send us after reviewing the site. Starter includes 1 round, Business includes 2 and Premium includes 3. Additional changes can be quoted separately.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your text and images, plus any logo or brand colors you already have. Delivery time (7, 14 or 21 days, depending on the package) starts on the day we receive your content.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every package includes a responsive layout that adapts to phone, tablet and desktop screens. The Premium package also includes a cross-browser testing report.

Can I add pages or features to a package?

Yes. If you need more pages, or a mix of features from different packages, use the custom quote button above and tell us what you need.
